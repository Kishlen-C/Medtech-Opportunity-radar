# Criteria and scoring rubric (put this on the main Notion page)

Edit every line. The agents read this page first and score against it, so vague criteria give vague scores.

## What I want
- Types: summer placements, industrial years, research studentships, competitions, hackathons
- Areas: <e.g. medical imaging, devices, rehab engineering, clinical engineering>
- Level / year of study: <...>
- Location: <...>
- Eligibility facts the agent may rely on: <leave blank if you do not want it to assume anything>

## Fit score (0-5)
- 5: matches type, area, level and location, open now or opening soon, no eligibility catch
- 4: strong match with one minor catch
- 3: worth knowing about (goes in the digest)
- 2: weak match
- 1: marginal
- 0: irrelevant

Only 3 and above appear in the weekly digest. Everything lower is still logged with Status Ignored so it is not re-evaluated.
